# Privacy Policy – Kanso

Kanso is a local music player designed to play audio files stored on your device.

## Data Collection

Kanso does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app does not require user accounts, and no information is transmitted to external servers.

## Device Permissions

Kanso requests access to audio files on your device solely to:

- Scan your music library
- Display metadata (artist, album, artwork)
- Enable playback of local audio files

Kanso does not access any other personal files or sensitive data.

## Data Sharing

Kanso does not share any user data with third parties.

## Analytics and Tracking

Kanso does not use analytics, tracking tools, or advertising services.

## Security

All data remains on your device. Kanso does not upload or process data externally.
